### anchor-launch

- **Tells**: anchor and problem → features acted out round it → a count → collapse and burst onto the claim → pull back into the mark.
- **Intent**: One piece of the mark holds still while worlds change round it, and the end reveals it was the mark.
- **Fits / does not fit**: Launches with a mark that has one separable piece and four to six features. Not: Screenshot demos; films with no mark.
- **Slots**:

  | Role | Seconds | Repeats | Scenes that fill it | Peak | Bed kind |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `hook` | 2–3.5 | 1 | anchor-rise | hook | intro |
  | `demo` | 2–3 | 2–6 | field-card | – | build |
  | `proof` | 2.5–3.5 | 1 | cell-count | – | build |
  | `climax` | 3–4.5 | 1 | burst-claim | claim | drop |
  | `lockup` | 4.5–6.5 | 1 | mark-pullback | lockup | outro |

- **Length**: 14–40 s; peaks 2–3; sound bed `press-bed`.
- **Example films**: the halftone template's example (the halftone demo film); a recomposed segment in the halftone recipe.
- **Source**: own (`assets/templates/halftone/content.js, timing.rules.js`), split from the halftone style of keynote-video. **Licence**: MIT (Filmsmith).
- **Sample**: `poster.jpg`, an English still 480 px wide.
