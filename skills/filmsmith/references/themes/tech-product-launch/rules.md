# Rules: tech-product-launch

Each rule cites the date of the owner's verdict it came from. Change a rule with a new verdict, or when it conflicts with the theory ([foundations.md](../../foundations.md#who-decides)); tell the owner before changing it.

1. **The information is the picture.** The product's real flow — steps, decisions, files, checks, counts — is drawn on screen as diagrams, tables, grids and connectors. A viewer can retell the flow after one watch. Metaphor footage may support a beat; it never replaces the flow. *(2026-10-16: three diagram-led styles accepted, the metaphor-led one ruled out.)*
2. **One accent colour, with one meaning.** The palette is neutral plus a single accent, and the accent marks one kind of thing throughout. The mode card's Accent role is authoritative; today it reads: phosphor green, what passed or is proven (narrowing pending, see its card); plakat red, the human and the human's decisions; blueprint amber, a person's annotation or approval (a machine's check is a line-colour stamp); ink, the dot and what it becomes; thread, the thread that carries the work from step to step. Status, category and effect colours are not accents and follow their own conditions (principle 7). *(2026-10-16: confirmed; 2026-10-18: the four colour roles accepted with the check of the seven modes.)*
3. **One through-line object or a persistent frame.** The whole film is one place (a terminal, a poster series, a drawing), and either an object carries the eye from the first frame to the last or a frame stays on screen throughout. A frame holds only real information: chapter, progress, brand, licence; never a decorative timecode, BPM or beat counter. The viewer always knows which chapter they are in; a mode with no frame shows the step another way (STEP n / N, a stage number). Where a mode card and this rule disagree, the card may use its through-line object instead of the persistent frame, and states why. *(2026-10-16: confirmed; 2026-10-18: frame limited to real information, through-line or frame, accepted with the check of the seven modes.)*
4. **Brisk rhythm; clean, purposeful effects.** Key elements land on beats, big type and counters carry the tempo; fast and slow passages alternate; the film never makes the viewer wait: a step holds still at most one bar, a page's last step one and a half, a page carries at most twelve words (principle 5, brisk). Effects are welcome when they are clean and mark a beat or a meaning (principle 4); none is a default, and flashes obey the flash gate. The arrangement stays restrained at the delivery level of the principles. *(2026-10-15: the first 42-second cut "had no tension" — it held flow cards too long; 2026-10-16: the owner does not dislike effects, the first bed was too loud; 2026-10-18: the owner likes clean, tidy effect transitions and did not mean that all flashes are welcome; after the seven re-rendered modes, every technical film is to be brisk, direct and clean, and clean is not plain — promoted to principles 5 and 6 because the owner stated it for all films.)*
5. **Borrow grammar, not layout.** A reference film may teach structure and devices; the finished film must not read as the same design side by side. *(2026-10-15: "担心被指责完全一致".)*
6. **Settle fonts with samples.** When the owner hesitates over a typeface, make about 5 seconds per candidate — same shot, copy, timing and sound, only the font changed — before applying one to the whole film. *(2026-10-15: "各做一个样品我看看" / "5s 就行".)*
