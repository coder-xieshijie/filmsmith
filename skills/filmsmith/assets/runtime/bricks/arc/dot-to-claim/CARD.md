### dot-to-claim

- **Tells**: who broke it → the size of the problem → blame → guess becomes method → the name → how it works → the number → the verdicts → the claim → the command.
- **Intent**: One dot, one gesture: from the problem to the claim the viewer follows a single mark.
- **Fits / does not fit**: a developer tool's launch with one claim, 20–45 s. Not: explainers, films without a name drop.
- **Slots**:

  | Role | Seconds | Repeats | Scenes | Peak | Bed |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | hook | 1.9–2.9 | 1 | word-swap (`first`) | hook | build |
  | problem | 1.9–2.9 | 1–3 | dot-slam, word-warp | – | build |
  | turn | 1.9–2.9 | 1 | dot-burst | – | build |
  | turn | 3.5–4 | optional | the bloom (recipe; template only) | – | build → silence |
  | climax | 3.5–4.5 | 1 | width-name | reveal (first drop) | drop |
  | demo | 2.5–4 | 1 | step-dots, film-ruler | – | drop |
  | proof | 1.9–4 | optional | count-stop | – | drop |
  | proof | 1.9–2.9 | optional | word-swap (`answer`) | – | rebuild |
  | claim | 2.4–3.5 | optional | claim-portal | climax (70–85 %) | second drop |
  | lockup | 3.9–6 | 1 | cursor-close | lockup | outro |

- **Climax**: the claim at 70–85 % (the example 78 %); peaks: the hook, the name, the claim (or the lock-up without a claim).
- **Example films**: the ink template's example (34.8 s at 124 BPM): swap 0–2.4, slam 2.4–4.8, slam (split) 4.8–7.3, warp 7.3–9.2, burst 9.2–11.6, bloom 11.6–15.5, name 15.5–19.4, steps 19.4–22.7, counter 22.7–24.7, swap 24.7–27.1, claim 27.1–30.0, cta 30.0–34.8.

- **Sample**: `poster.jpg`, an English still 480 px wide.
- **Pitfalls**: One claim per film; its whiteout is the film's one peak after the name.
