### reveal-open

- **Tells**: "here is a world; this is its name; and this is the one thing it is made of." Low energy first, the name second, the hero third, then the film's features take over.
- **Fits / does not fit**: the first 11–20 s of a product or launch film that names the product and then shows its atomic unit before the features. Not for films that open on a problem (put a `problem` slot first instead), fast-cut cold opens (`cold-open`), or films under about 20 s, where the hook and the name should be one scene (`word-window`, `orbit-title`, `icon-field` each carry both).
- **Slots**:

  | Role | Seconds | Scenes (tags) | Notes |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `hook` (peak `hook`, required) | 3–7 | `flow-landscape`, `icon-field`, `crane-reveal`, `orbit-title` | a world before anything is named, low energy; one image that says "many" or "flow" |
  | `claim` (peak `claim`, required) | 2.5–5 | `name-stamp`, `word-window`, `outline-write`, `segment-reveal`, `shard-gather` | the name stated once and held at least 1 s |
  | `turn` | 0.5–1.5 | — | on a bar line, into the hero |
  | `demo` (required) | 5–7 | `hero-spotlight`, `card-conjure` | one hero and one whole action arc (rise, hold, settle) of at least 3 s; the slowest, most textured shot |

  Length 11–20 s; one or two T3 peaks (the hook's and the claim's). The rules behind it: the opening shows one protagonist with a complete arc, not a crowd; a name or wordmark holds at least 30 frames; the opening's main action runs at least 3 s; at least two scene changes in the first 5 s, none longer than 6 s except the hero, which is the film's deliberate slow shot.
- **Climax**: none here; the fragment builds the film's first impression and hands the energy curve to the features (rising, alternating with rests) and the finale.
- **Example films**: a launch film: `flow-landscape` (6 s, many sources into one) → `name-stamp` (3 s, the name, held) → turn → `hero-spotlight` (5.75 s, the one card) → features. A short teaser: `icon-field` (6 s) → `word-window` (5.5 s, ends inside the product) → features.
