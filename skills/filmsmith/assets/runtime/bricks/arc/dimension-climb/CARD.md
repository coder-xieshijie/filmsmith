### dimension-climb

- **Tells**: point → line → plane → solid → interface → running console → mark.
- **Intent**: One shape climbs a dimension per scene, so the product reads as one continuous idea.
- **Fits / does not fit**: Developer products whose story is one thing becoming the next: request, response, spec, docs, a running call. Not: Data stories; products without a geometric mark.
- **Slots**:

  | Role | Seconds | Repeats | Scenes that fill it | Peak | Bed kind |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `hook` | 2.5–4 | 1 | point-line | hook | intro |
  | `demo` | 3.5–5 | 1–2 | pill-card | – | build |
  | `turn` | 3–4.5 | 1 | hex-solid | – | build |
  | `demo` | 2.5–4 | 1 | cube-page | – | build |
  | `demo` | 3–5 | 1 | panel-focus | – | build |
  | `climax` | 4.5–6.5 | 1 | run-flood | reveal | drop |
  | `lockup` | 4.5–6.5 | 1 | mark-command | lockup | outro |

- **Length**: 12–40 s; peaks 2–3; sound bed `glass-climb`.
- **Example films**: the morph template's example (the morph demo film); a recomposed segment in the morph recipe.
