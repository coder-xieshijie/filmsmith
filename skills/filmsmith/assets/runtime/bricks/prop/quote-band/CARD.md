### quote-band

- **Structure**: a band of `paper` across the sheet column (x 120–1616), 250 px (quotes, 69 px semibold, baselines +104 and +190) or 320 px (the hook, 96 px bold, +132 and +244), text inset 96 px; parts `stock`, `line`, `phrase`.
- **States**: `entering` (the first phrase rising), `quoted` (all phrases in), `hook` (the 320 px band).
- **Sizes**: S 960 × 540, M 1440 × 810, L 1920 × 1080 (the frame scaled).
- **Swap points**: `lines` (1–2 lines of phrases).
- **Intent**: Make a line of the page readable without retyping the page: paste the words over it.
- **Content**: 1–2 lines of phrases, the page's own words.
- **Parameters** and **Roles**: `lines`; roles ground, paper, onPaper.
- **Styles**: scroll; any film quoting a capture. **Source**: own (the source film). **Licence**: MIT (Filmsmith).
- **Languages**: en full · zh adapted: Noto Sans SC at the same sizes, no space between Chinese phrases.
- **Sample**: `poster.jpg`, an English still 480 px wide.
- **Pitfalls**: Never add a claim: cut words only.
