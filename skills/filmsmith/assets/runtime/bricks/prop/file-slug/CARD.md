### file-slug

- **Structure**: mono 400 25 (Chinese 28) in dim at y 54 + 26: the slug at x 96, the page right-aligned at x 1824 in a 900 px clip; parts `slug`, `page`.
- **States**: `before`, `changing` (the old page leaving up in 0.2 s, the new rising 0.1 s later over 0.3 s), `after`.
- **Sizes**: S 960 × 540, M 1440 × 810, L 1920 × 1080 (the frame scaled; the sample lowers it to the middle).
- **Swap points**: `slug`.
- **Intent**: Tell the viewer where in the file they are.
- **Content**: The file's name and the page in hand ("Exhibit 2 of 4", "Worksheet, from exhibit 4", "Index of exhibits", "4 exhibits").
- **Parameters** and **Roles**: `slug`, `ex`, `of`; roles ground, dim.
- **Styles**: dossier. **Source**: own (the dossier source film). **Licence**: MIT (Filmsmith).
