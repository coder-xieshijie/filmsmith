### page-read

- **Tells**: here is the page → what it says it does → what it covers → the proof inside it → how it is measured → its name.
- **Intent**: The page is the story: the film only reads it, in order, and opens it once.
- **Fits / does not fit**: a README, docs or changelog in order, 16–60 s. Not: many artefacts, app screens.
- **Slots**:

  | Role | Seconds | Repeats | Scenes | Peak | Bed |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | hook | 3–4.5 | 1 | sheet-open | hook | intro |
  | problem | 2.5–4 | 1–2 | quote-detent | – | build |
  | demo | 4–7 | optional | row-ratchet | – | build |
  | climax | 3.5–5 | 1 | row-unfold | reveal (55–80 %) | drop |
  | proof | 3–4 | optional | quote-detent (`reel`) | – | build |
  | lockup | 5–6.5 | 1 | roll-lockup | lockup | outro |

- **Climax** at 55–80 % (the example 63 %); peaks: the hook, the unfold, the lock-up.
- **Example films**: the scroll template's example (30 s at 120 BPM): open 0–4, quote 4–7, quote 7–10, ratchet 10–16, unfold 16–20, quote 20–24, lockup 24–30.
- **Source**: own (the source film). **Licence**: MIT (Filmsmith).
- **Sample**: `poster.jpg`, an English still 480 px wide.
- **Pitfalls**: One unfold, at the climax; never two in a row.
