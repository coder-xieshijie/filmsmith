### type-enter

- **Looks**: a title sets itself on screen piece by piece. `blur` (default): words one after another rise a little out of a blur and settle, the accent word last and slower, a small line under them after. `rise`: each character comes up from below its own line, a tiny overshoot, clipped so nothing shows below the line. `press`: words land one by one from 1.28× with a little blur, like a stamp. `drift`: characters fly in from all round the frame, tumbling and out of focus, and lock into place one by one with a small pulse; the finished word breathes once. `drop`: characters fall from above the frame onto a hairline floor, squash, bounce twice and snap still, each with a contact shadow. `path`: characters run along a curve across the frame, each turning with the curve, then lift off it into a straight line. `tumble`: characters fly in from deep behind the screen, turning on all three axes and out of focus while far, and land in reading order, the last one a fixed time after the first.
- **Tier**: T0 (`blur`, `rise`, `press`); `drift`, `drop`, `path`, `tumble` are T1. **Family**: mask-type. **Surface**: canvas.
- **Use**: the first appearance of a title, a claim or a label on an empty or quiet ground; `blur` is the quiet default for most titles, the other six are livelier; `tumble` suits the words of a page or board assembling itself after its shapes. **Not for**: text already on screen or already read (use a T0 change; re-entering reads as a stutter); more than two lines; more than two kinds of title entrance in a film, and `drift`, `drop`, `path` or `tumble` more than once.
- **Intent**: The title arrives piece by piece in reading order, so the viewer reads it as it lands; blur stays out of the way, the other ways make the arrival an event.
- **Content**: One or two lines of title (≤ 40 chars a line), an optional accent word and an optional sub line; for path, a curve that means something (a growth line).
- **Parameters**: `from` the ground (nothing of the text), `to` the set text. `how` `'blur'` | `'rise'` | `'press'` | `'drift'` | `'drop'` | `'path'` | `'tumble'`; `text` (a string or `{ en, zh }`, default the sample long title), `accent` (a substring drawn in the accent, slower in `blur`), `size` (en 140, zh 128), `weight` 700, `x` 960, `y` 540 (block centre), `align` `'center'`, `maxW` 1560, `sub` (`{ text, size, weight, x, y, align }`: a second line that blurs up after the title), `path` (four control points of the cubic curve, default from lower left to upper right), `land` (`tumble`: frames from `lead` until the last character has landed, default 70), `offsets` (`tumble`: per character `{ dx, dy, dz, rx, ry, rz }` in px and degrees in place of the seeded flight), `probes` (unit indexes whose offsets are added to `track()`, for the comparison). Units: English words (`blur`, `press`) or characters; Chinese phrases of 2–4 characters (`blur`, `press`) or characters. Roles read: `ink` (text), `accent` (accent word, `rise` rule, `path` curve), `line` (`drop` floor), `ground` (`drop` shadow).
- **Theory**: piecewise appearance gives reading order and pace (progressive disclosure); each unit's small settle is follow-through (Thomas & Johnston 1981); `drift` and `drop` turn the assembly into an event: scattered parts gathering into one figure (Gestalt common fate).
- **Styles**: any; `drop` and `drift` suit playful looks (plakat, pulse), avoid them in dossier and blueprint.
- **Length and easing**: 3 beats default, 1.5–8 from the number of units (a long title is squeezed to fit `beats` when set). `blur` gap 4 f, 22 f per unit (accent 30 f), `EASE.out`; `rise` gap en 2 f / zh 3 f, 14 f to a −10 % overshoot then 6 f; `press` gap 4 f, 9 f, cubic-bezier(.2, .75, .3, 1); `drift` gap en 3 f / zh 4 f, 45 f per character, lock pulse 8 f; `drop` fall 24 f, bounces 30 % and 9 %, snap 22 f after the last; `path` 60 f along the curve cubic-bezier(.42, 0, .12, 1), lift 18 f cubic-bezier(.3, 1.22, .6, 1); `tumble` 16 f per character `EASE.snappy`, characters `clamp((land − 16) / n, 0.35, 1.6)` f apart, seeded flight dx ±260, dy ±200, depth 200–760 px, turns ±170° / ±190° / ±120° under a 900 px perspective, opacity min(1, 1.8a), blur (1 − a)² × (1 + 7·depth / 760) px.
- **Sound**: `land` as the last unit settles (`drop`: a tick per landing), `turn` on the `path` lift.
- **Merged from**: `split-text-stagger` (`rise`), `paper-title-card` (`press`; its underline is `marker-stroke` `rule`, its digit roll is left to the counter bricks), `drift-assembly` (`drift`), `letter-drop-physics` (`drop`), `text-on-path` (`path`), `assemble-then-type-flyin` (`tumble`, its words; the shells and board are blueprint-build).

- **Languages**: en full · zh adapted: Chinese is set in phrases of 2–4 characters from `Intl.Segmenter` (lines break only between phrases), never by letter-spacing or case; per-character variants (`rise`, `drift`, `drop`, `path`) move each character, slower than English (gaps 3–4 f) so a two-character word does not flash; `path` runs Chinese upright along the curve with a smaller turn. Weakest in Chinese: `drift` and `path`, where single characters lose the word until they lock.
- **Sample**: `poster.jpg`, an English still 480 px wide.
- **Differs from upstream**: no paper grain, vignette, brand line or install command; the sub line is optional; `drift` directions are seeded per character (upstream fixed per index) and `drop` bounces are closed-form (no physics step), so their tracks differ by design (waived, see `compare/`); the `blur` default is the compared one. Compare (`blur`): pass (track 1.5 px, progress RMSE 0.005, events ±1 f, SSIM drop 0.065); the five variants `rise` to `path` are not compared (their upstream demos use React state and fixed per-index directions; their timing constants are copied). `tumble` is compared on its own (`compare/pairs-tumble.json`, the upstream's flights passed as `offsets`): pass (track 0.1 px, progress RMSE 0.0001, events ±0 f, SSIM drop 0.006); the CSS 3D turn is drawn as an affine map of the projected centre and axes, so a turned glyph is foreshortened but not keystoned, and spaces take no slot in the stagger (upstream they do).
- **Pitfalls**: At most two kinds of title entrance per film, counting type-retype and write-on; use blur for every title but the hero. Chinese must be split by meaning into 3–5 phrases, not into single characters. Over about 8 words, shorten the gap between words or the last one enters during the hold. Keep the camera still while drift, drop or path is flying; drop wants words without descenders (g, j, p, q, y).
